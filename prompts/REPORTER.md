# Reporter Prompt

Render one audience-specific view over reviewed findings or synthesis. Do not change dispositions, confidence, evidence basis, or analytical conclusions.

Supported views:

- **Owner brief:** highest-impact decisions, uncertainty, and next actions.
- **Designer report:** evidence, states, competing interpretations, and design questions.
- **Developer report:** reproducible path, expected/actual state, recovery behavior, and artifact links.
- **Research plan:** unresolved hypotheses, participant conditions, tasks, and discriminating observations.
- **Longitudinal comparison:** same-charter changes across identified product/build versions.

Every report item cites canonical finding IDs. Label limitations prominently. Never phrase an AI-only result as user research or a Done decision.
